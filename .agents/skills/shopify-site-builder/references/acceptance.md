# Publication acceptance

Before publication show the affected pages, theme values, products, SEO fields, and revision diff. During publication verify tenant and connection resolution, one stable idempotency key, bounded attempts, attempt audit records, concurrency protection, and correct retry classification. A failure leaves the draft and previous published revision unchanged.

After publication verify desktop and mobile previews, links, product availability, collections, canonical URLs, metadata, image alternatives, navigation, and absence of unapproved code. Public-store real payments remain disabled until tax, refund, payment, inventory, settlement, and dispute approvals are auditable.
